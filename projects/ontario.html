What this data cannot show

  • This is a list of projects the province chose to publish, not all infrastructure spending.
  • Budgets are estimated totals over a project's life, not money spent to date, and a third of projects publish none. Category and region totals are lower bounds.
  • Budget coverage varies from 98% for child care to 20% for recreation, so comparisons between categories are unreliable.
  • Region totals are not adjusted for population.
