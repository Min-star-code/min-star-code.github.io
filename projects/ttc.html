Method and data quality

The analysis joins incident codes to the City's published cause descriptions, parses dates and times, and standardizes line aliases such as YUS and LINE 1.

Zero-minute incidents, missing line labels, multi-line labels and unmatched cause codes all stay visible. 145 records have codes absent from the description table; they are labelled as unmapped and no cause is guessed.

Invalid dates, times or negative delays would be excluded and counted. The 2025 snapshot has zero excluded records. Both input files are verified against SHA-256 checksums before each run.

What this data cannot show

  • Delay minutes are summed from individual incident rows. There is no passenger-level delay, train-service volume or other denominator for a reliability rate.
  • A cause code does not prove why a specific passenger was delayed.
  • 16,642 of 25,737 incidents recorded zero delay minutes. They count as incidents and add nothing to minutes, which is why the two measures rank causes differently.
  • 354 incidents are logged against more than one line and are kept as their own group.
