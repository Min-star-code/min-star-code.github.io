How the numbers are defined

Eight source tables are loaded into SQLite. The model keeps shipped orders only and joins each of the 121,317 order lines to the product's standard cost on its order date. On 64 lines no cost interval matches, so the current standard cost is used and flagged.

  • Revenue is line revenue after the unit-price discount, excluding tax and freight.
  • Estimated gross profit is revenue minus quantity times standard cost. It is not net profit.
  • Gross margin is estimated gross profit divided by revenue.

The Excel dashboard imports six SQL exports through Power Query and reconciles its totals to the SQL controls on a QA sheet. Seventeen Python tests cover the pipeline.

What this data cannot show

  • AdventureWorks is Microsoft's fictional sample, not a real business. Orders run from 30 May 2022 to 29 June 2025.
  • Gross profit uses standard cost and excludes operating expenses and returns.
  • Discount-band comparisons are observational; channel mix and product mix could explain some of the difference.
  • Order and customer counts are left out of this page because they cannot be added across categories.
