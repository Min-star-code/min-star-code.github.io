How every project is built.

Three rules I kept across all four.

No row is dropped

Records that fail a check are flagged and kept. The cleaned Ontario file has the same 5,935 rows as the source, and all 7,029 outbreak records survive the merge.

Totals are checked twice

Excel totals are matched back to SQL, the outbreak data runs through 12 validation rules, and the AdventureWorks pipeline has 17 automated tests.

Limits are written down

Each report says what the data cannot support: budgets are lower bounds, outbreak counts are not case counts, and delay totals are not a reliability rate.
